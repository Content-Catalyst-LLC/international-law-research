# Research Note: Civil and Political Rights in International Law

This article analyzes civil and political rights as a legal architecture for limiting public power and protecting participation, liberty, equality, dignity, and fair process.

Core source clusters:

1. UN Charter and Universal Declaration foundations.
2. ICCPR and Optional Protocol procedures.
3. Human Rights Committee general comments and views.
4. Regional human rights systems.
5. Limitation, derogation, non-discrimination, and remedy doctrines.
6. Contemporary challenges including surveillance, emergency powers, borders, occupation, protest, and digital governance.
