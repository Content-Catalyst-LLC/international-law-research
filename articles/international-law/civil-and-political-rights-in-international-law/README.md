# Civil and Political Rights in International Law

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **Civil and Political Rights in International Law**.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — publication metadata.
- `data/` — primary authorities, further reading, and structured matrices.
- `python/` — small reproducible helpers for matrix and link inventories.
- `sql/` — lightweight schema and sample queries for article research data.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON outputs from the helper scripts.
