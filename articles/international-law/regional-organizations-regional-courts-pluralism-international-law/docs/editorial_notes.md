# Editorial Notes

This article follows **International Organizations and the Legal Authority of Global Institutions** and precedes **International Arbitration and the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes** in the International Law series.

Editorial posture:

- Treat regional organizations as legal actors, not merely political clubs.
- Treat regional courts as sites of doctrine, access, and contestation.
- Avoid assuming universal institutions are always superior to regional systems.
- Emphasize the double edge of pluralism: legal innovation and legal fragmentation.
- Connect regionalism to Global South agency, postcolonial legal development, and unequal institutional capacity.
