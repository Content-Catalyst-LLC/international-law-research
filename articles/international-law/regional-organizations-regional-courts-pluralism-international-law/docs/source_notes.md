# Source Notes

Primary sources emphasize official institutional and treaty materials:

- UN Charter Chapter VIII.
- European Convention on Human Rights and ECtHR materials.
- EU/CJEU institutional materials.
- OAS Charter, American Convention, and Inter-American Court materials.
- African Charter, African Court Protocol, African Union materials.
- ECOWAS treaty and court materials.
- ASEAN Charter.

Secondary sources emphasize international adjudication, legal pluralism, regional human-rights systems, institutional authority, and international legal fragmentation.
