# Regional Organizations, Regional Courts, and Pluralism in International Law

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst article **Regional Organizations, Regional Courts, and Pluralism in International Law**.

The article examines how regional organizations, regional courts, and regional legal regimes shape international law through security cooperation, human-rights adjudication, economic integration, treaty interpretation, institutional practice, and plural legal authority.

## WordPress article

The copy-ready WordPress HTML is stored at:

```text
docs/wordpress_article_html.html
```

## Research/data files

- `data/primary_sources.csv` — core treaties, charters, statutes, and institutional authorities.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — scholarly and reference sources.
- `data/regional_institution_matrix.csv` — comparison of regional institutions and legal functions.
- `data/regional_court_matrix.csv` — comparison of major regional courts and tribunals.
- `data/regional_pluralism_examples.csv` — applied examples of regional pluralism, fragmentation, and complementarity.
- `docs/doctrine_summary.md` — doctrinal overview.
- `docs/source_notes.md` — source-selection and research notes.
- `docs/editorial_notes.md` — editorial positioning and article-map placement.
