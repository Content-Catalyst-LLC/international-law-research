# Investment Treaties, Investor-State Dispute Settlement, and Regulatory Sovereignty

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article on investment treaties, investor-state dispute settlement, and regulatory sovereignty.

The folder is intentionally a research/support package only. It does not contain the full WordPress article HTML. The live article belongs in WordPress; this repository keeps structured source notes, metadata, matrices, lightweight code, and generated research outputs.

## Contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — article title, slug, SEO metadata, series placement, and footer navigation metadata.
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — core treaties, arbitration rules, institutional materials, and leading authorities.
- `sources/further_reading.md` — deeper books, reports, reform materials, and scholarship.
- `data/*.csv` — structured matrices for doctrines, procedures, regulatory-sovereignty risks, and reform pathways.
- `sql/schema.sql` — simple schema for loading the article research data.
- `python/build_outputs.py` — local helper that summarizes CSV matrices into JSON and Markdown outputs.
- `outputs/` — generated summaries and a Catalyst Data export row.

## Repository policy

Full WordPress article HTML is not stored in GitHub. GitHub functions as the research archive, not as a parallel publication copy.
