# Further Reading

- Dolzer, R. and Schreuer, C. (2022) *Principles of International Investment Law*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Sornarajah, M. (2021) *The International Law on Foreign Investment*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
- Van Harten, G. (2007) *Investment Treaty Arbitration and Public Law*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Puig, S. and Shaffer, G. (2018) 'Imperfect Alternatives: Institutional Choice and the Reform of Investment Law', *American Journal of International Law*.
- Roberts, A. (2018) 'Incremental, Systemic, and Paradigmatic Reform of Investor-State Arbitration', *American Journal of International Law*.
- UNCTAD, *World Investment Report* and IIA reform notes.
- ICSID caseload statistics and institutional reform materials.
- UNCITRAL Working Group III materials on ISDS reform.
