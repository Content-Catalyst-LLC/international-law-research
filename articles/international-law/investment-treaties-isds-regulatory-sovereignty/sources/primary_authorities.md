# Primary Authorities

- Convention on the Settlement of Investment Disputes between States and Nationals of Other States, 1965 (ICSID Convention).
- ICSID Arbitration Rules and Regulations.
- UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules.
- UNCITRAL Rules on Transparency in Treaty-based Investor-State Arbitration, 2014.
- United Nations Convention on Transparency in Treaty-based Investor-State Arbitration, 2014 (Mauritius Convention on Transparency).
- Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, 1958 (New York Convention).
- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties, 1969.
- Energy Charter Treaty, 1994, and modernization / withdrawal materials.
- UNCTAD International Investment Agreements Navigator and IIA reform materials.
- OECD materials on investment treaties and responsible business conduct.
- Selected investment awards addressing fair and equitable treatment, indirect expropriation, national treatment, MFN, jurisdiction, damages, counterclaims, and regulatory measures.
