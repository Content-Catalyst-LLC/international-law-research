# Further Reading

- Fidler, D.P. (1999) *International Law and Infectious Diseases*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Fidler, D.P. (2004) *SARS, Governance and the Globalization of Disease*. Basingstoke: Palgrave Macmillan.
- Burci, G.L. and Vignes, C.H. (2004) *World Health Organization*. The Hague: Kluwer Law International.
- Toebes, B. (1999) *The Right to Health as a Human Right in International Law*. Antwerp: Intersentia.
- Gostin, L.O., Habibi, R. and Meier, B.M. (2020) 'Has global health law risen to meet the COVID-19 challenge?', *The Lancet*.
- Meier, B.M. and Gostin, L.O. (eds.) (2018) *Human Rights in Global Health: Rights-Based Governance for a Globalizing World*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Eccleston-Turner, M. and Brassington, I. (eds.) (2021) *Infectious Diseases in the New Millennium: Legal and Ethical Challenges*. Cham: Springer.
- Taylor, A.L. and Habibi, R. (2020) 'The collapse of global cooperation under the WHO International Health Regulations at the outset of COVID-19', *ASIL Insights*.
- Hoffman, S.J. and Røttingen, J.A. (2015) 'Assessing implementation mechanisms for an international agreement on research and development for health products', *Bulletin of the World Health Organization*.
