# Primary Authorities

- World Health Organization (1946) *Constitution of the World Health Organization*.
- World Health Organization (2005, as amended) *International Health Regulations*.
- World Health Assembly (2025) *WHO Pandemic Agreement*.
- World Health Organization (2011) *Pandemic Influenza Preparedness Framework for the Sharing of Influenza Viruses and Access to Vaccines and Other Benefits*.
- United Nations (1966) *International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights*, especially Article 12.
- Committee on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (2000) *General Comment No. 14: The Right to the Highest Attainable Standard of Health*.
- United Nations (1948) *Universal Declaration of Human Rights*, especially Article 25.
- United Nations General Assembly (2015) *Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development*.
- World Trade Organization (1994) *Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights*.
- World Trade Organization (2001) *Doha Declaration on the TRIPS Agreement and Public Health*.
- Food and Agriculture Organization, World Organisation for Animal Health, United Nations Environment Programme and World Health Organization (2021-present) *One Health Joint Plan of Action and related materials*.
