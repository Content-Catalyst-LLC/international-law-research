# Editorial Notes

## Article role in the series

This article opens the specialized/emerging-domain cluster after the economic-law sequence. It links international law to pandemic response, public-health coordination, institutional authority, equity, human rights, and scientific uncertainty.

## Key framing

- International health law is not only about emergencies. It includes preparedness, surveillance, equity, medicines, institutional capacity, human rights, and cross-border coordination.
- The International Health Regulations are central but not sufficient by themselves.
- Pandemic governance exposes the tension between sovereignty and shared vulnerability.
- Health equity, vaccine access, technology transfer, and financing are legal and political questions, not merely charitable policy concerns.
- One Health connects human, animal, and environmental health governance.
