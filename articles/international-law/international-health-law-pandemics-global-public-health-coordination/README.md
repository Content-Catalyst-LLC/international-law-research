# International Health Law, Pandemics, and Global Public Health Coordination

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst article **International Health Law, Pandemics, and Global Public Health Coordination**.

The package contains research and support materials only. It intentionally does **not** store a full WordPress HTML copy of the article.

## Scope

The article examines international health law as a field of global coordination, emergency governance, institutional authority, human rights protection, equity, and pandemic preparedness. It focuses on WHO authority, the International Health Regulations, pandemic emergencies, surveillance and notification, core capacities, travel and trade measures, access to vaccines and medicines, pathogen sharing, One Health, financing, and the politics of global public health coordination.

## Repository contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — article metadata and series placement
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — core legal and institutional authorities
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly and institutional further reading
- `data/*.csv` — structured article matrices
- `sql/health_law_schema.sql` — simple schema for the article support data
- `python/build_health_law_outputs.py` — local helper for generated summaries
- `outputs/*.json` — generated article-support indexes
- `notes/editorial_notes.md` — editorial and workflow notes

## WordPress / GitHub policy

WordPress remains the live publication layer. GitHub stores supporting research architecture, source notes, metadata, matrices, and reproducible outputs only.
