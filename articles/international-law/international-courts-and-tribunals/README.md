# International Courts and Tribunals

Companion research folder for the International Courts and Tribunals article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- ICJ, ICC, ITLOS, ECHR, WTO, ICSID, ICTY, ICTR, and hybrid tribunal source metadata
- jurisdiction, consent, advisory opinions, enforcement, and compliance concept mapping
- Palestine and South Africa v. Israel adjudication notes
- Black internationalism and international law from below notes
- selective justice and unequal access notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for international adjudication metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
