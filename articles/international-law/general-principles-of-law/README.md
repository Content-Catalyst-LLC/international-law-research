# General Principles of Law

Companion research folder for the General Principles of Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the General Principles of Law article, including:

- Article 38(1)(c) source metadata
- ILC general-principles concept mapping
- comparative-law and international-system formation notes
- legal-source quote logs
- SQL schema for general-principles research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
