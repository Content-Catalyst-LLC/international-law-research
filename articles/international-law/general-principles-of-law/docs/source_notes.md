## Critical framing

General principles should not be treated as a neutral universal vocabulary. The category has historically been shaped by unequal legal traditions, empire, colonial hierarchy, and selective recognition. A contemporary account should preserve the doctrinal value of general principles while expanding the comparative horizon beyond historically dominant European legal systems.
