# Climate Change Law, Treaties, and the Legal Politics of Decarbonization

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst International Law article on climate change law, treaty architecture, and the legal politics of decarbonization.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — article metadata, SEO fields, tags, and series placement.
- `data/primary_authorities.csv` — core treaties, decisions, institutions, and advisory proceedings.
- `data/further_reading.csv` — secondary sources and institutional research.
- `data/climate_law_architecture.csv` — treaty and governance framework matrix.
- `data/decarbonization_governance_matrix.csv` — decarbonization issue matrix.
- `data/legal_politics_risk_matrix.csv` — risk/dispute workflow matrix.
- `sql/climate_change_law_reference_schema.sql` — lightweight reference schema.
- `python/build_outputs.py` — local output builder for summary artifacts.
- `outputs/` — generated article summary and source inventory.
