# Lawyer Workflow Notes: ICC and Global Justice

1. Identify the situation, alleged conduct, victims, actors, and temporal frame.
2. Test Rome Statute jurisdiction before evaluating merits.
3. Separate subject-matter jurisdiction from admissibility and complementarity.
4. Distinguish legal validity of warrants from practical enforcement likelihood.
5. Assess cooperation, evidence access, witness protection, chain of custody, and digital verification.
6. Address selectivity and legitimacy critiques without substituting them for legal analysis.
