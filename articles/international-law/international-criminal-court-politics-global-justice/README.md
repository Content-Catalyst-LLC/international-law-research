# The International Criminal Court and the Politics of Global Justice

This article folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law series article **The International Criminal Court and the Politics of Global Justice**.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — article metadata for publication workflows.
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — treaty, institutional, and tribunal source anchors.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — selected scholarly and institutional references.
- `data/icc_institutional_architecture.csv` — ICC structure and political-legal functions.
- `data/icc_admissibility_complementarity_matrix.csv` — jurisdiction, admissibility, and complementarity checks.
- `data/icc_cooperation_enforcement_matrix.csv` — cooperation, arrest, enforcement, and legitimacy constraints.
- `python/build_icc_matrices.py` — builds consolidated JSON outputs.
- `python/audit_links.py` — audits links in the HTML article.
- `sql/create_icc_tables.sql` — example SQL schema for the article data.
