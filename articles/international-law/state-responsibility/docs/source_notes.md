## Critical framing

The article should explain ARSIWA precisely while refusing to treat state responsibility as politically neutral. It should emphasize that responsibility doctrine can be used by weaker states, occupied peoples, postcolonial states, and marginalized communities to contest impunity, while also acknowledging that invocation and enforcement remain deeply unequal.
