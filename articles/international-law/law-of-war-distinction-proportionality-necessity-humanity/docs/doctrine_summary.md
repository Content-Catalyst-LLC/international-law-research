# Doctrine Summary

The law of war regulates how parties conduct hostilities once an armed conflict exists. Its core operational principles are distinction, proportionality, precautions, military necessity, and humanity. These principles do not legalize the resort to force; they limit the conduct of armed conflict regardless of which party is responsible for starting the war.

Key analytical sequence:

1. Classify the conflict and applicable legal regime.
2. Identify protected persons and objects.
3. Determine whether a proposed target is a lawful military objective.
4. Evaluate expected incidental civilian harm.
5. Compare that harm against concrete and direct military advantage.
6. Assess feasible precautions and warnings.
7. Examine special protections for medical units, humanitarian relief, cultural property, and persons hors de combat.
8. Consider responsibility, command responsibility, and war-crimes exposure.
