# The Law of War: Distinction, Proportionality, Necessity, and Humanity

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst International Law article on the core operational principles of the law of war: distinction, proportionality, military necessity, humanity, precautions, civilian protection, and accountability.

## Folder contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — full WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — article metadata for repository and Catalyst Data mapping.
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — treaty, customary-law, and institutional primary authorities.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — selected books, commentaries, and research references.
- `data/law_of_war_principles_matrix.csv` — principle-by-principle doctrinal map.
- `data/targeting_rules_matrix.csv` — targeting and precautions analysis table.
- `data/war_crimes_matrix.csv` — selected war-crimes and accountability mapping.
- `python/build_law_of_war_matrices.py` — builds structured JSON outputs from article CSV data.
- `python/audit_links.py` — local URL extraction and audit helper.
- `python/update_catalyst_exports_for_article.py` — appends/updates lightweight Catalyst Data export rows.
- `sql/queries.sql` — example queries for structured article data.
- `outputs/article_summary.json` — article summary metadata.

## Series context

This article is part of the International Law knowledge series, specifically the Use of Force and International Humanitarian Law cluster.
