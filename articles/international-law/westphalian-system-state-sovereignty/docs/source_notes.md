## Critical framing

The article should treat Westphalia as both historical event and jurisprudential symbol. It should avoid claiming that modern sovereignty was fully created in 1648. It should explain the myth, its utility, and its limits, while foregrounding empire, colonization, unequal recognition, Indigenous and colonized peoples, and the later transformation of sovereign equality through decolonization and the UN Charter system.
