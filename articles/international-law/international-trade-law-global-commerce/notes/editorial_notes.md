# Editorial Notes

## Placement in the International Law series

This article opens the international economic law cluster after the environmental and climate-law sequence. It should function as the broad trade-law foundation before the investment-law and development/inequality articles.

## Navigation

## GitHub content rule

The full WordPress HTML article should not be stored in this repository. This folder is a research-support package only.

## Emphasis

The article should avoid presenting trade law as merely technical market law. Its framing should show how global commerce is legalized through WTO agreements, dispute settlement, domestic implementation, market-access commitments, development politics, and regulatory-autonomy conflicts.
