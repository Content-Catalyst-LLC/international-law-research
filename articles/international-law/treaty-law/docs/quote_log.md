# Quote Log — Treaty Law

## Current quote candidates

### Vienna Convention, Article 2(1)(a)

Excerpt: “an international agreement concluded between States in written form and governed by international law”

Editorial purpose: Defines treaty status and explains why legal effect matters more than the instrument’s label.

### Vienna Convention, Article 6

Excerpt: “Every State possesses capacity to conclude treaties.”

Editorial purpose: Anchors treaty-making capacity in state legal personality.

### Vienna Convention, Article 26

Excerpt: “Every treaty in force is binding upon the parties to it and must be performed by them in good faith.”

Editorial purpose: Explains pacta sunt servanda and the good-faith foundation of treaty performance.

### Vienna Convention, Article 27

Excerpt: “may not invoke the provisions of its internal law as justification for its failure to perform a treaty”

Editorial purpose: Explains the difference between domestic legal process and international responsibility.

### Vienna Convention, Article 31(1)

Excerpt: “ordinary meaning” — “context” — “object and purpose”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the general rule of treaty interpretation.

### Vienna Convention, Article 19(c)

Excerpt: “incompatible with the object and purpose of the treaty”

Editorial purpose: Explains the limit on treaty reservations.

### Vienna Convention, Article 52

Excerpt: “procured by the threat or use of force”

Editorial purpose: Explains coercion of a state as a ground of treaty invalidity.

### Vienna Convention, Article 53

Excerpt: “a norm from which no derogation is permitted”

Editorial purpose: Explains jus cogens as a limit on treaty freedom.

### UN Charter, Article 102(1)

Excerpt: “registered with the Secretariat and published by it”

Editorial purpose: Explains treaty registration as a public-order mechanism.
