## Critical framing

Treaty law should not be presented as a neutral doctrine of formal consent alone. It should also account for unequal bargaining power, colonial treaty-making, protectorates, imposed settlements, economic dependency, sanctions pressure, development asymmetry, and the historical gap between formal legal equality and real negotiating power.
