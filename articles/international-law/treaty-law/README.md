# Treaty Law

Companion research folder for the Treaty Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the Treaty Law article, including:

- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties source metadata
- treaty-law concepts and article mapping
- legal-source quote logs
- SQL schema for treaty-law research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
