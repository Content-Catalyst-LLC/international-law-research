## Treaty Law Concepts

- **Treaty definition** (Article 2(1)(a)) — Treaty as an international agreement in written form governed by international law.
- **Capacity to conclude treaties** (Article 6) — Every state possesses capacity to conclude treaties.
- **Consent to be bound** (Articles 11-17) — Legal methods by which states express consent to treaty obligation.
- **Entry into force** (Article 24) — Rules governing when treaty obligations become legally operative.
- **Pacta sunt servanda** (Article 26) — Treaties in force bind parties and must be performed in good faith.
- **Internal law and observance of treaties** (Article 27) — Internal law normally cannot justify failure to perform treaty obligations.
- **Treaty interpretation** (Articles 31-33) — Ordinary meaning context object and purpose supplementary means and multilingual texts.
- **Reservations** (Articles 19-23) — Unilateral statements excluding or modifying legal effect of treaty provisions.
- **Invalidity and coercion** (Articles 48-52) — Grounds of invalidity including error fraud corruption and coercion.
- **Jus cogens** (Article 53) — Peremptory norms limiting treaty freedom.
- **Amendment suspension and termination** (Articles 39-41 and 54-64) — Rules for changing suspending terminating or withdrawing from treaties.
- **Material breach** (Article 60) — Serious treaty breach and its consequences.
- **Fundamental change of circumstances** (Article 62) — Restricted doctrine allowing treaty exit in exceptional transformed circumstances.
- **Successive treaties** (Article 30) — Rules for treaties relating to the same subject matter.
