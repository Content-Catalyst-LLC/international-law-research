# The Future of International Law in an Age of Fragmentation, Multipolarity, and Planetary Crisis

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article:

**The Future of International Law in an Age of Fragmentation, Multipolarity, and Planetary Crisis**

This is the final article in the International Law series.

The folder is intentionally limited to research and support materials. It does **not** store the full WordPress HTML article. WordPress remains the publication layer; GitHub remains the research, metadata, source, and structured-output layer.

## Contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — title, slug, sequence, SEO metadata, and repository status.
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — primary instruments, institutional materials, cases, and advisory-opinion materials.
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly and institutional reading list in Harvard style with authoritative links where possible.
- `data/future_domains_matrix.csv` — major future-facing domains of international law.
- `data/scenario_framework_matrix.csv` — possible future legal-order scenarios.
- `data/planetary_crisis_matrix.csv` — planetary-risk legal questions.
- `data/lawyer_workflow_matrix.csv` — future-oriented lawyer-facing analytical workflow.
- `sql/future_international_law_schema.sql` — simple schema for cataloguing future-facing international law issues.
- `python/build_future_international_law_outputs.py` — builds structured JSON support outputs from the CSV files.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON summaries and indexes.

## Editorial note

This article concludes the International Law series. It treats the future of international law not as simple collapse or linear progress, but as a redistribution of legal authority across treaties, courts, regional systems, soft law, technical standards, domestic litigation, corporate governance, social movements, and planetary-risk regimes.
