# Editorial Notes

This article is the concluding piece in the Sustainable Catalyst International Law series.

The framing should avoid two oversimplifications:

1. International law is not simply collapsing.
2. International law is not simply progressing.

The core argument is that international legal authority is being redistributed across formal and informal sites: treaties, courts, advisory opinions, regional systems, domestic litigation, soft law, technical standards, corporate due diligence, procurement, finance, sanctions, social movements, and planetary-risk governance.

The article should preserve a lawyer-facing orientation while also functioning as a reflective conclusion to the entire series.
