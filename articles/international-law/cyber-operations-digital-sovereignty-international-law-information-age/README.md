# Cyber Operations, Digital Sovereignty, and International Law in the Information Age

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst article **Cyber Operations, Digital Sovereignty, and International Law in the Information Age**.

The package contains research and support materials only. It intentionally does **not** store a full WordPress HTML copy of the article.

## Scope

The article examines how international law applies to cyber operations and digital governance, including sovereignty, jurisdiction, non-intervention, use of force, due diligence, attribution, state responsibility, countermeasures, critical infrastructure, election interference, human rights, cybercrime, private platform power, cloud infrastructure, AI-enabled cyber activity, and the application of international humanitarian law to cyber conflict.

## Repository contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — article metadata and series placement
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — core UN, treaty, institutional, and interpretive authorities
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly and institutional further reading
- `data/*.csv` — structured article matrices
- `sql/cyber_operations_schema.sql` — simple schema for the article support data
- `python/build_cyber_operations_outputs.py` — local helper for generated summaries
- `outputs/*.json` — generated article-support indexes
- `notes/editorial_notes.md` — editorial and workflow notes

## WordPress / GitHub policy

WordPress remains the live publication layer. GitHub stores supporting research architecture, source notes, metadata, matrices, and reproducible outputs only.
