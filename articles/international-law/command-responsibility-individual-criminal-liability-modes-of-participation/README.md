# Command Responsibility, Individual Criminal Liability, and Modes of Participation

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **Command Responsibility, Individual Criminal Liability, and Modes of Participation**.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML
- `metadata.json` — article metadata, SEO fields, slug, tags, and navigation context
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — primary legal authorities
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — scholarship and institutional commentary
- `data/liability_modes_matrix.csv` — modes of participation and liability concepts
- `data/command_responsibility_matrix.csv` — superior-responsibility elements and proof questions
- `data/evidence_linkage_matrix.csv` — evidence categories used to connect accused persons to crimes
- `python/build_command_responsibility_matrices.py` — local matrix/export helper
- `python/audit_links.py` — local non-network URL inventory helper
- `sql/command_responsibility_schema.sql` — lightweight schema for the article's structured data
- `outputs/` — generated JSON/Markdown outputs

## Repository role

The article connects international criminal law doctrine to practical legal analysis: actus reus, mens rea, Rome Statute modes of liability, superior responsibility, omissions, effective control, knowledge, failure to prevent or punish, and evidentiary linkage.
