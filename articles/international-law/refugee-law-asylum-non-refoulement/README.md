# Refugee Law, Asylum, and the Principle of Non-Refoulement

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article on refugee law, asylum, and the principle of non-refoulement.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — article metadata, SEO fields, slug, excerpt, and tags.
- `data/primary_authorities.csv` — core treaties, institutional materials, and case authorities.
- `data/further_reading.csv` — Harvard-style secondary and institutional reading list.
- `data/refugee_law_framework_matrix.csv` — key doctrine, institutions, and legal questions.
- `data/non_refoulement_analysis_matrix.csv` — treaty and human-rights non-refoulement analysis pathways.
- `data/protection_pathways_matrix.csv` — asylum, complementary protection, temporary protection, and durable solutions.
- `python/build_refugee_law_outputs.py` — local helper that builds JSON outputs from CSV matrices.
- `python/link_inventory.py` — local helper that extracts links from the article HTML.
- `sql/refugee_law_schema.sql` — lightweight schema for structured research tables.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON and link inventory outputs.
