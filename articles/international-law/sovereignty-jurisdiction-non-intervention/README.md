# Sovereignty, Jurisdiction, and Non-Intervention in International Law

Companion research folder for the Sovereignty, Jurisdiction, and Non-Intervention article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UN Charter Article 2 source metadata
- Friendly Relations Declaration source notes
- Lotus jurisdiction materials
- Nicaragua non-intervention materials
- Palestine advisory-opinion and UNGA ES-10/24 materials
- jurisdiction, coercion, sovereignty, and non-intervention concept mapping
- Western impunity and selective-enforcement notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for sovereignty/jurisdiction/non-intervention metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
