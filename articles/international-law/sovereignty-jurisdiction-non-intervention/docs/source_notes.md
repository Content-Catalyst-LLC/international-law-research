## Critical framing

The article should explain doctrine precisely while refusing to sanitize the history of sovereign inequality. It should foreground the formal legal protection of sovereignty and non-intervention while showing how powerful states have repeatedly violated or diluted these principles in practice, especially in Latin America, Africa, West Asia, and Palestine.
