# Law of the Sea / UNCLOS

Companion research folder for the Law of the Sea / UNCLOS article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UNCLOS source metadata and maritime-zone concept mapping
- territorial sea, contiguous zone, EEZ, continental shelf, and high-seas notes
- common heritage of humankind and International Seabed Authority notes
- Commission on the Limits of the Continental Shelf materials
- ITLOS and UNCLOS dispute-settlement notes
- BBNJ / High Seas Treaty materials
- South China Sea Arbitration and North Sea Continental Shelf case notes
- marine environment, climate, fisheries, seabed mining, biodiversity, and equity notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for law-of-the-sea research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
