## Critical framing

The article should present the law of the sea as one of the most sophisticated legal regimes in international law while foregrounding ecological crisis, distributive justice, maritime hierarchy, coastal-state rights, high-seas freedoms, militarized navigation, fisheries pressure, deep seabed mining, Global South participation, landlocked states, small island developing states, and the tension between ocean commons and extractive capitalism.
