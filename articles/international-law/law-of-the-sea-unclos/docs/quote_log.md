# Quote Log — Law of the Sea / UNCLOS

Use this file to track short primary-source excerpts used in the article.

## Current quote candidates

### UNCLOS Preamble

Excerpt: “all issues relating to the law of the sea”

Editorial purpose: Shows the Convention’s comprehensive ambition.

### UNCLOS Article 2

Excerpt: “sovereignty of a coastal State extends, beyond its land territory and internal waters”

Editorial purpose: Anchors territorial-sea sovereignty.

### UNCLOS Article 17

Excerpt: “ships of all States, whether coastal or land-locked, enjoy the right of innocent passage”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the navigational compromise inside the territorial sea.

### UNCLOS Article 33

Excerpt: “contiguous to its territorial sea, described as the contiguous zone”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the contiguous-zone regime.

### UNCLOS Article 56

Excerpt: “sovereign rights for the purpose of exploring and exploiting, conserving and managing the natural resources”

Editorial purpose: Anchors EEZ resource jurisdiction.

### UNCLOS Article 76

Excerpt: “continental shelf of a coastal State comprises the seabed and subsoil”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the continental-shelf regime.

### UNCLOS Article 87

Excerpt: “The high seas are open to all States”

Editorial purpose: Anchors high-seas freedom.

### UNCLOS Article 136

Excerpt: “The Area and its resources are the common heritage of mankind”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the common-heritage principle.

### UNCLOS Article 192

Excerpt: “States have the obligation to protect and preserve the marine environment.”

Editorial purpose: Anchors environmental protection.

### UNCLOS Article 287

Excerpt: “choose, by means of a written declaration, one or more of the following means”

Editorial purpose: Anchors dispute-settlement options.

### BBNJ Agreement Article 2

Excerpt: “to ensure the conservation and sustainable use of marine biological diversity of areas beyond national jurisdiction”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the High Seas Treaty’s object and purpose.
