## Law of the Sea Concepts

- **Law of the sea** (field) — Legal regime governing ocean space maritime zones navigation resources and environmental protection.
- **UNCLOS** (core treaty) — Comprehensive convention governing ocean space uses and resources.
- **Baselines** (maritime measurement) — Lines from which maritime zones are measured.
- **Territorial sea** (maritime zone) — Coastal-state sovereignty extending up to 12 nautical miles subject to innocent passage.
- **Innocent passage** (navigation regime) — Right of foreign ships to pass through the territorial sea if passage is not prejudicial to peace good order or security.
- **Contiguous zone** (maritime zone) — Zone up to 24 nautical miles for enforcement of customs fiscal immigration and sanitary laws.
- **Exclusive economic zone** (maritime zone) — Zone up to 200 nautical miles where coastal states have sovereign rights over resources.
- **Continental shelf** (maritime zone) — Seabed and subsoil entitlement tied to natural prolongation and UNCLOS criteria.
- **Extended continental shelf** (continental shelf) — Shelf entitlement beyond 200 nautical miles based on Article 76 and CLCS process.
- **High seas** (maritime commons) — Ocean areas beyond national jurisdiction open to all states under UNCLOS freedoms.
- **Common heritage of humankind** (deep seabed principle) — Principle governing the Area and its mineral resources for the benefit of humankind as a whole.
- **International Seabed Authority** (institution) — UNCLOS body organizing and controlling mineral-resources activities in the Area.
- **Marine environmental protection** (obligation) — UNCLOS duty to protect and preserve the marine environment.
- **BBNJ Agreement** (implementation treaty) — Agreement under UNCLOS for biodiversity beyond national jurisdiction.
- **ITLOS** (dispute settlement) — Independent judicial body established by UNCLOS.
- **Annex VII arbitration** (dispute settlement) — Default compulsory procedure for many UNCLOS disputes.
- **South China Sea Arbitration** (case) — Annex VII arbitration clarifying maritime entitlements and environmental obligations under UNCLOS.
- **Climate and sea-level rise** (contemporary challenge) — Ocean warming acidification sea-level rise and shifting baselines affecting maritime law.
- **Equitable benefit sharing** (equity principle) — Distribution of seabed or marine genetic resource benefits with attention to developing states.
- **Landlocked and geographically disadvantaged states** (equity category) — States with special interests in ocean access resources and benefit sharing.
