# International Human Rights Law: Foundations, Covenants, and Contemporary Development

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **International Human Rights Law: Foundations, Covenants, and Contemporary Development**.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — SEO, slug, tags, series navigation, and article metadata.
- `data/primary_authorities.csv` — official treaties, declarations, courts, and institutional materials.
- `data/further_reading.csv` — Harvard-style scholarly and institutional reading list.
- `data/human_rights_framework_matrix.csv` — foundations, covenants, institutions, and contemporary-development map.
- `data/rights_categories_matrix.csv` — rights categories, instruments, and legal functions.
- `data/accountability_mechanisms_matrix.csv` — treaty bodies, regional courts, Charter mechanisms, and domestic implementation routes.
- `python/build_human_rights_matrices.py` — generates JSON outputs from CSV matrices.
- `python/audit_links.py` — extracts article links for a lightweight link inventory.
- `sql/human_rights_schema.sql` — simple schema for importing the article matrices.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON and link inventory files.

## Repository role

The folder is designed to keep the article's research materials auditable without turning the public article into a technical appendix.
