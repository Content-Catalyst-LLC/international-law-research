# International Environmental Law and the Governance of Planetary Risk

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **International Environmental Law and the Governance of Planetary Risk**.

It contains the WordPress-ready HTML, metadata, authority lists, environmental-law matrices, SQL schema, helper scripts, and generated outputs used to make the article’s research workflow transparent.

## Repository contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — full article HTML for WordPress.
- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — title, slug, SEO metadata, article sequence, tags, and excerpt.
- `references/primary_authorities.csv` — treaty, declaration, tribunal, and institutional materials.
- `references/further_reading.csv` — scholarly and institutional secondary sources.
- `data/environmental_law_framework_matrix.csv` — principles, duties, and legal functions.
- `data/planetary_risk_governance_matrix.csv` — risk domains, legal tools, and governance challenges.
- `data/environmental_principles_matrix.csv` — prevention, precaution, due diligence, EIA, cooperation, and participation.
- `sql/schema.sql` — simple article-support schema.
- `python/build_article_outputs.py` — output builder.
- `python/link_inventory.py` — article link extractor.
- `outputs/` — generated summary, inventory, and Catalyst Data row.
