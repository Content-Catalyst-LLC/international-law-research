## Critical framing

Custom should not be presented as neutral repetition. The article should examine who historically counted as a state, whose practice was recorded, whose protest was legible, whose silence was treated as acquiescence, and how colonialism, technological asymmetry, naval power, military capacity, and institutional authority shaped customary-law formation.
