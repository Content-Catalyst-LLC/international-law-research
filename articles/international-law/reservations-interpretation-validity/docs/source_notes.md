## Critical framing

Reservations, interpretation, and validity should be treated as doctrinal tools and as sites of power. The article should explain black-letter treaty law while also examining how reservations can fragment common obligations, how interpretation can become a site of institutional and geopolitical contestation, and how validity doctrine reveals that formal consent does not always settle the legality of treaty obligation.
