# Humanitarian Intervention, Responsibility to Protect, and the Limits of Sovereignty

This article folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst International Law article on humanitarian intervention, the responsibility to protect, and the limits of sovereignty.

## Purpose

The folder provides structured source metadata, doctrinal matrices, legal-authority mapping, and editorial documentation for the public WordPress article. It is designed to support transparent research, reproducible source organization, and compatibility with the broader Catalyst Data export layer.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — copy-ready WordPress article HTML.
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — primary legal instruments, UN materials, Security Council resolutions, and major cases.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — scholarly and treatise sources.
- `data/r2p_pillars_matrix.csv` — R2P pillar structure and legal function.
- `data/humanitarian_intervention_framework.csv` — doctrinal framework for intervention claims.
- `data/case_matrix.csv` — leading case and situation matrix.
- `python/build_matrices.py` — builds JSON outputs from the CSV matrices.
- `python/audit_links.py` — local URL audit helper.
- `python/update_catalyst_exports.py` — updates repo-level Catalyst Data export CSVs.
- `sql/queries.sql` — example SQL queries for Catalyst Data-style analysis.

## Series Context

This article follows **Self-Defense, Collective Security, and the UN Charter Framework** and precedes **International Humanitarian Law: The Geneva Conventions and the Regulation of Armed Conflict**.
