## Editorial frame

Treat secession and recognition as legal doctrines shaped by power, consent, self-determination, territorial integrity, and the uneven politics of recognition. Avoid flattening contested cases into propaganda. Distinguish legal criteria, recognition practice, UN membership, effectiveness, legitimacy, and justice.
