# Editorial Notes: International Humanitarian Law: The Geneva Conventions and the Regulation of Armed Conflict

## Article purpose

This article introduces international humanitarian law as the legal framework regulating armed conflict after hostilities begin. It connects Geneva treaty law, customary IHL, conflict classification, protected persons, conduct of hostilities, occupation, humanitarian relief, accountability, and the relationship between IHL and human rights.

## Style notes

Maintain the distinction between jus ad bellum and jus in bello. Avoid implying that IHL legitimizes unlawful resort to force. Keep the tone serious, institutionally grounded, and lawyer-facing.
