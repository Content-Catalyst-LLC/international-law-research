# Quote Log — Jus Cogens and Erga Omnes

Use this file to track short primary-source excerpts used in the article.

## Current quote candidates

### Vienna Convention, Article 53

Excerpt: “a norm from which no derogation is permitted”

Editorial purpose: Defines the non-derogable character of jus cogens.

### Vienna Convention, Article 64

Excerpt: “any existing treaty which is in conflict with that norm becomes void and terminates”

Editorial purpose: Explains the effect of a newly emerging peremptory norm.

### Barcelona Traction

Excerpt: “all States can be held to have a legal interest in their protection”

Editorial purpose: Defines the erga omnes structure of legal interest.

### Belgium v. Senegal

Excerpt: “common interest in compliance”

Editorial purpose: Explains erga omnes partes obligations within a treaty community.

### ILC 2022 Draft Conclusions

Excerpt: “accepted and recognized by the international community of States as a whole”

Editorial purpose: Identifies the recognition requirement for peremptory status.

### ILC 2022 Annex

Excerpt: “prohibition of aggression” — “prohibition of genocide” — “prohibition of slavery” — “right of self-determination”

Editorial purpose: Records the non-exhaustive list of peremptory norms previously referred to by the ILC.

### ICJ 2024 Palestine Advisory Opinion

Excerpt: “not to recognize as legal the situation arising from the unlawful presence”

Editorial purpose: Explains non-recognition duties of third states in a contemporary setting.
