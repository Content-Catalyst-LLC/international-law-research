## Critical framing

The article should treat jus cogens and erga omnes as doctrines of hierarchy and community obligation, not merely as abstract source-law categories. It should connect doctrine to anti-domination struggles, including anti-colonialism, anti-apartheid, abolition, self-determination, resistance to aggressive war, and the rights of peoples under occupation.
