# Lawyer Workflow — Universal Jurisdiction and Accountability for Atrocity Crimes

1. Identify the alleged crime category and domestic implementing law.
2. Confirm whether the forum recognizes universal jurisdiction for that crime.
3. Separate investigation, indictment, arrest, trial, extradition, and sentencing thresholds.
4. Analyze suspect presence, nationality, immunity, and extradition constraints.
5. Map evidence sources, chain of custody, witness protection, and mutual legal assistance needs.
6. Compare universal jurisdiction with ICC, territorial-state, and hybrid accountability options.
7. Test selectivity and legitimacy objections without treating political difficulty as legal impossibility.
