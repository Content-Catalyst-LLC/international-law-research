# Universal Jurisdiction and Accountability for Atrocity Crimes

This repository folder supports the WordPress article **Universal Jurisdiction and Accountability for Atrocity Crimes** in the International Law knowledge series.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — full WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata.json` — article metadata for publishing and indexing.
- `data/` — primary authorities, secondary literature, and practice matrices.
- `python/` — local helpers for matrix export and URL audit.
- `sql/` — schema starter for structured analysis tables.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON and CSV summaries produced by helper scripts.

## Research framing

The article treats universal jurisdiction as a decentralized accountability mechanism for grave international crimes, while emphasizing its dependence on domestic procedure, evidence, custody, mutual legal assistance, prosecutorial discretion, immunity doctrine, and political legitimacy.
