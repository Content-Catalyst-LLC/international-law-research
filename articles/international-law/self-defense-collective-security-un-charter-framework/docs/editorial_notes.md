# Editorial Notes

This article continues the International Law use-of-force cluster after the article on the prohibition on the use of force.

Editorial priorities:

- Keep Article 51 narrow and disciplined.
- Distinguish self-defense from retaliation, reprisal, prevention, and punishment.
- Treat collective security as the Charter framework, not as a synonym for military alliances.
- Address Security Council authorization and veto politics without treating enforcement paralysis as legal permission for unilateral force.
- Maintain a lawyer-facing analytical structure grounded in jurisdiction, evidence, authority, and consequences.
