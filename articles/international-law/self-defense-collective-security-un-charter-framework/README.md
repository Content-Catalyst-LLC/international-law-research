# Self-Defense, Collective Security, and the UN Charter Framework

This article folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst International Law article on self-defense, collective security, and the UN Charter framework.

The folder contains source metadata, doctrinal matrices, case references, article HTML, editorial notes, and lightweight scripts for local link auditing and structured output generation.

## Scope

The article examines Article 51 of the United Nations Charter, the relationship between self-defense and collective security, the Security Council framework, the requirement of armed attack, necessity, proportionality, immediacy, collective self-defense, reporting obligations, anticipatory and preventive self-defense claims, non-state actors, proxy force, cyber operations, drones, occupation, and selective enforcement.
