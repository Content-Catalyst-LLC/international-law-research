# Nuremberg, Tokyo, and the Historical Development of International Criminal Justice

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article:

- WordPress HTML: `docs/wordpress_article_html.html`
- Article metadata: `metadata.json`
- Primary authorities: `data/primary_sources.csv`
- Further reading / secondary sources: `data/secondary_sources.csv`
- Historical development matrix: `data/nuremberg_tokyo_development_matrix.csv`
- Tribunal comparison matrix: `data/tribunal_comparison_matrix.csv`
- Doctrine legacy matrix: `data/doctrine_legacy_matrix.csv`
- Reproducible outputs: `outputs/`

The materials are intended to support structured research on Nuremberg, Tokyo, the postwar trials, ad hoc tribunals, hybrid courts, and the historical development of international criminal justice.
