# Historical International Criminal Justice Memo Workflow

1. Identify the forum and legal basis.
2. Map the crimes charged and their historical formulation.
3. Distinguish trial record, legal doctrine, and political context.
4. Identify procedural guarantees and fairness critiques.
5. Track doctrinal migration into later tribunal or ICC law.
6. Address selectivity, legality, and victor's justice counterarguments.
7. Conclude with the legal legacy and institutional limits.
