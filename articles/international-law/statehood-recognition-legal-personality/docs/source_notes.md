## Critical framing

The article should explain doctrine precisely while refusing to treat legal existence as politically neutral. Statehood and recognition have historically been shaped by empire, colonization, racial hierarchy, unequal recognition, occupation, partition, and selective institutional admission. Palestine should be treated as a central contemporary case, not a marginal example.
