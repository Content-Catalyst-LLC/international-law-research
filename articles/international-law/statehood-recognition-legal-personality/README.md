# Statehood, Recognition, and Legal Personality

Companion research folder for the Statehood, Recognition, and Legal Personality article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- Montevideo Convention source metadata
- UN Charter statehood and membership source notes
- recognition, non-recognition, and legal personality concept mapping
- United Nations non-member observer State notes
- Palestine UN status and membership materials
- Kosovo advisory opinion notes
- UN international legal personality materials
- quote logs
- SQL schema for statehood-recognition research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
