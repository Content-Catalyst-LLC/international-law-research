## Critical framing

The article should clarify the distinction between public international law and private international law without treating private international law as merely technical. Conflict of laws shapes access to justice, corporate accountability, family life, migration, labor, digital commerce, enforcement of judgments, and the real-world operation of cross-border legal systems. The article should show both the doctrinal distinction and the increasing overlap between state-centered public law and private transnational legal ordering.
