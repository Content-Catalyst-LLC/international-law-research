## Core Concepts

- **Public international law** (field) — Law governing states international organizations peoples and public international obligations.
- **Private international law** (field) — Conflict-of-laws rules governing cross-border private disputes.
- **Conflict of laws** (field) — Rules addressing jurisdiction applicable law and recognition/enforcement.
- **Article 38 sources** (source framework) — ICJ Statute formulation of public international law sources.
- **Treaties** (public source) — Written international agreements governed by international law.
- **Customary international law** (public source) — General practice accepted as law.
- **General principles** (public source) — Principles recognized across legal systems and used in international adjudication.
- **Jurisdiction** (private/public bridge) — Authority to hear and decide disputes or prescribe law across borders.
- **Applicable law** (private law question) — Question of which legal system governs a dispute.
- **Recognition and enforcement** (private law question) — Whether a foreign judgment will be recognized and enforced.
- **HCCH** (institution) — International organization developing private international law instruments.
- **Hague Service Convention** (private law treaty) — Cross-border service of judicial and extrajudicial documents.
- **Hague Choice of Court Convention** (private law treaty) — Exclusive choice of court agreements in civil or commercial matters.
- **Hague Judgments Convention** (private law treaty) — Recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments in civil or commercial matters.
- **International commercial arbitration** (private dispute mechanism) — Contract-based dispute resolution administered by institutions or ad hoc tribunals.
- **Investor-state dispute settlement** (hybrid mechanism) — Claims by private investors against states under investment treaties or contracts.
- **ICSID** (institution) — World Bank-affiliated center for conciliation and arbitration of investment disputes.
- **ICC arbitration** (institutional arbitration) — International Chamber of Commerce-administered arbitration.
- **Public/private overlap** (critical concept) — Interaction of state-centered law and private transnational dispute systems.
- **Access to justice** (critical lens) — Practical ability of parties to bring defend and enforce cross-border claims.
