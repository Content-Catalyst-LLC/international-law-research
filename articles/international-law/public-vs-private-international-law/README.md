# Public vs Private International Law

Companion research folder for the Public vs Private International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- public international law source metadata
- private international law / conflict-of-laws source metadata
- Article 38 and UN Charter notes
- HCCH Service Convention, Choice of Court Convention, and Judgments Convention materials
- ICSID and ICC arbitration notes
- comparative doctrine tables
- quote logs
- SQL schema for public/private international law metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
