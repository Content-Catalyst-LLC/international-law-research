# Economic, Social, and Cultural Rights in International Law

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **Economic, Social, and Cultural Rights in International Law**.

It contains the WordPress HTML article, metadata, primary authority tables, further reading tables, economic/social/cultural rights matrices, accountability mechanism matrices, a small SQL schema, Python helper scripts, and generated outputs.

## Article folder

`articles/international-law/economic-social-cultural-rights-in-international-law`

## WordPress HTML

`docs/wordpress_article_html.html`

## Core themes

- ICESCR architecture
- progressive realization
- maximum available resources
- minimum core obligations
- non-retrogression
- non-discrimination
- work, labor, social security, food, water, housing, health, education, culture, science
- domestic implementation and justiciability
- austerity, debt, sanctions, climate, corporate power, and digital systems
