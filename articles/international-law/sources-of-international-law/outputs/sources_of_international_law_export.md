## Source-Law Concepts

- **Article 38** (source framework) — Classical source framework of the ICJ Statute identifying treaties custom general principles and subsidiary means.
- **Treaties** (primary source) — Written international agreements governed by international law.
- **Customary international law** (primary source) — General practice accepted as law.
- **State practice** (custom evidence) — Conduct and official positions relevant to customary international law.
- **Opinio juris** (custom evidence) — Acceptance that a practice is carried out as a matter of legal right or obligation.
- **General principles of law** (primary source) — Principles recognized across legal systems or formed within the international legal system.
- **Judicial decisions** (subsidiary means) — Court and tribunal decisions used to determine rules of law.
- **Teachings of publicists** (subsidiary means) — Scholarly writings used as subsidiary means in legal analysis.
- **Soft law** (normative development) — Non-binding instruments that may shape expectation interpretation and future legal development.
- **Institutional practice** (normative development) — Practice of international organizations and treaty bodies influencing legal interpretation and development.
- **Jus cogens** (hierarchy) — Peremptory norms from which no derogation is permitted.
- **Erga omnes** (hierarchy) — Obligations owed to the international community as a whole.
- **Source interaction** (method) — Treaty custom principles judicial reasoning institutional practice and hierarchy interacting in legal analysis.
- **Colonial critique** (critical method) — Historical critique of whose practice principles and writings counted as international.
