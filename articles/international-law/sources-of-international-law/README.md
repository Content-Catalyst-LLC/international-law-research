# Sources of International Law

Companion research folder for the Sources of International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the Sources of International Law article, including:

- Article 38 source metadata
- treaties, custom, general principles, subsidiary means, soft law, and hierarchy notes
- source-category concept mapping
- short legal excerpt quote logs
- SQL schema for source-law research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
