# CBDR Doctrine Matrix

| concept | legal_location | core_question | burden_sharing_function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common but differentiated responsibilities | Rio Principle 7 | How should shared ecological responsibility be allocated across unequal states? | Frames common responsibility while recognizing unequal contribution and capacity. |
| CBDR-RC | UNFCCC Article 3 | How should climate duties account for equity and respective capabilities? | Connects climate action to responsibility capacity and development context. |
| Dynamic differentiation | Paris Agreement Article 2(2) | How does differentiation work when all parties submit NDCs? | Moves from fixed Annex categories toward national circumstances and progression. |
| Historical responsibility | Rio UNFCCC climate justice practice | Who contributed most to cumulative environmental harm? | Supports stronger duties for states with large historical emissions or extraction benefits. |
| Respective capabilities | UNFCCC and Paris practice | Who has the resources institutions and technology to act? | Links responsibility to economic technological and administrative capacity. |
| Special circumstances | UNFCCC Paris SIDS LDC practice | Which states face distinctive vulnerability or capacity constraints? | Supports adaptation finance flexibility and special support for vulnerable states. |
