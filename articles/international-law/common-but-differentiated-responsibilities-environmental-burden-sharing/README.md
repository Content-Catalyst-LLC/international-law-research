# Common but Differentiated Responsibilities and the Politics of Environmental Burden Sharing

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst article on common but differentiated responsibilities, environmental burden sharing, climate justice, historical responsibility, capacity, finance, loss and damage, and differentiated legal duties in international environmental law.

The repository intentionally does **not** store the full WordPress article HTML. WordPress remains the publication layer; GitHub stores structured research materials, metadata, authorities, matrices, and reproducible outputs.

## Folder contents

- `metadata/` — article metadata and Catalyst Data export row.
- `sources/` — primary authorities and further reading.
- `data/` — doctrine, burden-sharing, finance, and legal-politics matrices.
- `docs/` — editorial and research notes only, not article HTML.
- `python/` — local output builder.
- `sql/` — schema for structured source and doctrine records.
- `outputs/` — generated summaries for audit and reuse.

## Article scope

The article examines Rio Principle 7, UNFCCC Article 3, the Kyoto Protocol, Paris Agreement Article 2(2), equity, common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities, historical emissions, capacity, climate finance, technology transfer, adaptation, loss and damage, transparency, trade tensions, and the politics of environmental burden sharing.
