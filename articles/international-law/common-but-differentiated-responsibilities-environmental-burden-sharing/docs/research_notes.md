## Core analytical frame

Common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities, often abbreviated as CBDR-RC, is not simply a technical allocation formula. It is the legal vocabulary through which international environmental law tries to reconcile shared ecological responsibility with unequal histories, capacities, vulnerabilities, development needs, and institutional power.

## Lawyer-facing questions

- What legal instrument contains the relevant differentiation language?
- Is differentiation expressed as binding obligation, procedural expectation, financial commitment, reporting flexibility, capacity-building duty, or political principle?
- Which variables matter: historical responsibility, current emissions, capability, vulnerability, development need, or special circumstances?
- Does the framework distinguish mitigation, adaptation, finance, technology transfer, loss and damage, transparency, or compliance?
- How does the claim interact with human rights, trade law, investment law, debt, development finance, or domestic implementation?
