# Compliance, Enforcement, and the Limits of International Adjudication

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article on compliance, enforcement, and the limits of international adjudication.

## Scope

The article examines how international judgments, advisory opinions, provisional measures, criminal cooperation duties, human-rights rulings, trade compliance mechanisms, arbitral awards, and domestic implementation pathways become legally and politically effective — or fail to do so.

## Core themes

- ICJ judgments and Article 94 of the UN Charter
- Provisional measures and interim protection
- Advisory opinions and third-state legal consequences
- ICC cooperation, arrest, and non-cooperation
- Regional human-rights compliance supervision
- WTO compliance and retaliation
- Arbitral award enforcement under the New York Convention and ICSID Convention
- Domestic courts as enforcement sites
- Non-recognition and non-assistance duties
- Selective enforcement and power asymmetry
