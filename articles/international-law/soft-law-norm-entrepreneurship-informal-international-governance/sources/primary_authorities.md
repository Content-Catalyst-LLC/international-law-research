# Primary Authorities and Institutional Materials

- United Nations General Assembly (1948) *Universal Declaration of Human Rights*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1970) *Declaration on Principles of International Law concerning Friendly Relations and Co-operation among States in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1992) *Rio Declaration on Environment and Development*.
- United Nations General Assembly (2015) *Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development*.
- United Nations General Assembly (2024) *Global Digital Compact*.
- United Nations Human Rights Council (2011) *Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights: Implementing the United Nations “Protect, Respect and Remedy” Framework*.
- OECD (2023) *OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises on Responsible Business Conduct*.
- International Labour Organization (1998) *Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work*.
- ILO (1977, amended 2022) *Tripartite Declaration of Principles concerning Multinational Enterprises and Social Policy*.
- UN Global Compact (2000–present) *Ten Principles of the United Nations Global Compact*.
- International Law Commission (2018) *Conclusions on Identification of Customary International Law*.
- ISO (various) international standards relevant to governance, risk management, information security, environmental management, and organizational responsibility.
- Financial Stability Board, Basel Committee, FATF, IOSCO, and other informal or technical governance bodies whose standards influence national regulation.
