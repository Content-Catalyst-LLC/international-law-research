# International Organizations and the Legal Authority of Global Institutions

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article **International Organizations and the Legal Authority of Global Institutions**.

The article examines how international organizations acquire and exercise legal authority through constituent instruments, delegated powers, implied powers, institutional practice, technical expertise, treaty administration, privileges and immunities, and responsibility rules.

## Folder contents

- `metadata.json` — article metadata for WordPress, SEO, navigation, and Catalyst Data mapping.
- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — full WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `docs/doctrine_summary.md` — concise legal doctrine summary.
- `docs/source_notes.md` — notes on primary and secondary sources.
- `docs/editorial_notes.md` — editorial and citation notes.
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — primary authorities and institutional materials.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — scholarship and treatise references.
- `data/institution_authority_matrix.csv` — institutional-authority concepts and examples.
- `data/organization_profiles.csv` — selected organization profiles.
- `python/audit_links.py` — basic URL inventory/audit helper.
- `python/build_institution_matrix.py` — produces structured JSON from the matrix CSV.
- `sql/queries.sql` — example SQL queries for Catalyst Data-style analysis.
- `outputs/article_summary.json` — article summary metadata.
