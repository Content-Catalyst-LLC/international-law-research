# Source Notes

Primary sources emphasize constituent instruments and authoritative institutional law materials: the UN Charter, ICJ advisory opinions, specialized-agency instruments, privileges and immunities conventions, and the ILC Draft Articles on Responsibility of International Organizations.

The core doctrinal source is *Reparation for Injuries Suffered in the Service of the United Nations* (ICJ, 1949), especially for legal personality and implied powers.

Specialized-agency sources should be read alongside the relevant founding treaty or constitution. Institutional web pages are useful for description, but legal analysis should return to the constituent instrument, treaty text, and official legal materials wherever possible.

Secondary sources include major treatises and research handbooks on international organizations law, general international law, and institutional legal authority.
