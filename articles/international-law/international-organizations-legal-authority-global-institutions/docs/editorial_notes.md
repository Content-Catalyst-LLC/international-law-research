# Editorial Notes

This article is designed as a deep-dive International Law article for Sustainable Catalyst.

Editorial emphasis:

- Treat international organizations as legal actors, not merely policy forums.
- Distinguish binding decisions, soft law, technical standards, guidance, treaty administration, and political declarations.
- Connect doctrine to institutional practice and accountability concerns.
- Keep the analysis lawyer-facing without becoming a narrow law-school outline.
- Preserve the critical Sustainable Catalyst frame: international institutions make law operational, but their authority remains constrained by mandate, states, power, technocracy, and uneven accountability.
