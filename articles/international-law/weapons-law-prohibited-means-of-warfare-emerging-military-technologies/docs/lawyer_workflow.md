# Lawyer Workflow

1. Identify the weapon, means, method, platform, payload, software component, and operational concept.
2. Check treaty status, customary restrictions, national implementing law, and transfer restrictions.
3. Apply distinction, proportionality, precautions, unnecessary suffering, humanity, and environmental protection rules.
4. Review technical performance data, foreseeable effects, failure modes, civilian dependencies, and reverberating harm.
5. Determine whether lawful use depends on constraints, training, human authorization, target type, geography, or time.
6. Preserve audit records: legal review, target file, approvals, warnings, logs, after-action reports, and incident investigations.
