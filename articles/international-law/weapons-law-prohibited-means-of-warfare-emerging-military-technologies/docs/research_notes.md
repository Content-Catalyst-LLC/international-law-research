# Research Notes: Weapons Law, Prohibited Means of Warfare, and Emerging Military Technologies

This article treats weapons law as a bridge between traditional IHL principles and contemporary technical governance. It emphasizes that legal analysis does not end with naming a weapon category. Lawyers must examine design, effects, controllability, user judgment, foreseeable civilian harm, post-use remnants, environmental consequences, and accountability records.

Key doctrinal anchors include Hague law, Additional Protocol I, treaty prohibitions, customary IHL, Article 36 reviews, and war-crimes accountability.
