# International Space Law and the Governance of Outer Space

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst article **International Space Law and the Governance of Outer Space**.

The package contains research and support materials only. It intentionally does **not** store a full WordPress HTML copy of the article.

## Scope

The article examines international space law as a governance framework for outer space, celestial bodies, orbital activity, commercial space operations, satellites, launch responsibility, liability, registration, space debris, radio spectrum and orbital slots, remote sensing, space resources, dual-use systems, anti-satellite threats, planetary protection, equity, and the future of space governance.

## Repository contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — article metadata and series placement
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — core treaty, institutional, and soft-law authorities
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly and institutional further reading
- `data/*.csv` — structured article matrices
- `sql/space_law_schema.sql` — simple schema for the article support data
- `python/build_space_law_outputs.py` — local helper for generated summaries
- `outputs/*.json` — generated article-support indexes
- `notes/editorial_notes.md` — editorial and workflow notes

## WordPress / GitHub policy

WordPress remains the live publication layer. GitHub stores supporting research architecture, source notes, metadata, matrices, and reproducible outputs only.
