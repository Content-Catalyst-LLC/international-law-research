# Primary Authorities

- United Nations (1967) *Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies*.
- United Nations (1968) *Agreement on the Rescue of Astronauts, the Return of Astronauts and the Return of Objects Launched into Outer Space*.
- United Nations (1972) *Convention on International Liability for Damage Caused by Space Objects*.
- United Nations (1975) *Convention on Registration of Objects Launched into Outer Space*.
- United Nations (1979) *Agreement Governing the Activities of States on the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies*.
- United Nations Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS) (2019) *Guidelines for the Long-term Sustainability of Outer Space Activities*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1963) *Declaration of Legal Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1986) *Principles Relating to Remote Sensing of the Earth from Outer Space*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1992) *Principles Relevant to the Use of Nuclear Power Sources in Outer Space*.
- United Nations General Assembly (1996) *Declaration on International Cooperation in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space for the Benefit and in the Interest of All States*.
- International Telecommunication Union (ITU) *Radio Regulations* and satellite network coordination materials.
- Inter-Agency Space Debris Coordination Committee (IADC) *Space Debris Mitigation Guidelines*.
- NASA and partner states (2020-present) *Artemis Accords: Principles for Cooperation in the Civil Exploration and Use of the Moon, Mars, Comets, and Asteroids for Peaceful Purposes*.
