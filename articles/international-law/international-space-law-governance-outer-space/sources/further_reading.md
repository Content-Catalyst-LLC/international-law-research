# Further Reading

- Cheng, B. (1997) *Studies in International Space Law*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Lyall, F. and Larsen, P.B. (2018) *Space Law: A Treatise*. 2nd edn. London: Routledge.
- von der Dunk, F.G. and Tronchetti, F. (eds.) (2015) *Handbook of Space Law*. Cheltenham: Edward Elgar.
- Hobe, S., Schmidt-Tedd, B. and Schrogl, K.U. (eds.) (2009-present) *Cologne Commentary on Space Law*. Cologne: Carl Heymanns.
- Jakhu, R.S., Pelton, J.N. and Nyampong, Y.O.M. (eds.) (2017) *Space Mining and Its Regulation*. Cham: Springer.
- Tronchetti, F. (2009) *The Exploitation of Natural Resources of the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies*. Leiden: Brill Nijhoff.
- Freeland, S. and Jakhu, R.S. (eds.) (2016) *The Relationship Between Space Law and International Humanitarian Law*. Selected writings and proceedings.
- Gabrynowicz, J.I. (2004) *Space law: Its cold war origins and challenges in the era of globalization*. Suffolk University Law Review.
- Secure World Foundation (annual) *Global Counterspace Capabilities Report* and space sustainability materials.
- United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (n.d.) *Space Law: Treaties and Principles*.
