# Source Notes — International Arbitration and the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes

Primary authorities are organized around the peaceful-settlement provisions of the UN Charter, the PCA/Hague tradition, UNCLOS Annex VII, UNCITRAL instruments, the New York Convention, and ICSID.

Secondary sources include leading arbitration treatises, international dispute-settlement texts, and investment-law commentary. The source layer is designed for Catalyst Data compatibility: primary sources use `source_id,source_title,source_type,institution,year,url,notes`; secondary sources use `source_id,author,year,title,publisher,url,notes`.
