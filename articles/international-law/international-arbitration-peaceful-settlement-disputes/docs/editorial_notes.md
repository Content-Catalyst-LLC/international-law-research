# Editorial Notes — International Arbitration and the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes

This article is positioned after the regional organizations article and before the compliance/enforcement article in the International Law sequence. It should function as the bridge between institutional dispute-settlement forums and the later question of what happens after a decision, award, judgment, or recommendation is issued.

## Editorial emphasis

- Keep arbitration connected to Article 33 peaceful settlement rather than treating it only as private commercial procedure.
- Distinguish state-to-state arbitration, UNCLOS arbitration, investor-state arbitration, and commercial arbitration.
- Preserve the article's legitimacy critique: arbitration can resolve disputes peacefully, but access and design can also privilege capital, repeat players, and technical elites.
