# Editorial Notes

This article fills the missing International Law sequence slot between:

- Regional Organizations, Regional Courts, and Pluralism in International Law
- Compliance, Enforcement, and the Limits of International Adjudication

The article should be treated as a full dispute-settlement article because international arbitration is a distinct legal tradition rather than a subsection of courts or compliance.

Key emphasis:

- peaceful settlement under the UN Charter;
- consent and jurisdiction;
- compromis and compromissory clauses;
- Permanent Court of Arbitration;
- interstate arbitration;
- mixed claims commissions;
- investor-state arbitration;
- ICSID;
- UNCITRAL rules;
- commercial arbitration involving public-law concerns;
- awards, annulment, recognition, enforcement, and compliance;
- transparency, legitimacy, public interest, and reform.
