# International Arbitration and the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article:

**International Arbitration and the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes**

This article was inserted into the International Law sequence between:

1. **Regional Organizations, Regional Courts, and Pluralism in International Law**
2. **Compliance, Enforcement, and the Limits of International Adjudication**

The folder is intentionally limited to research and support materials. It does **not** store the full WordPress HTML article. WordPress remains the publication layer; GitHub remains the research, metadata, source, and structured-output layer.

## Contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — title, slug, SEO metadata, sequence placement, and repository status.
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — primary instruments, conventions, institutions, arbitral materials, and official sources.
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly reading list in Harvard style with authoritative links where possible.
- `data/arbitration_forms_matrix.csv` — major forms of international arbitration.
- `data/consent_jurisdiction_matrix.csv` — consent sources and jurisdictional questions.
- `data/procedure_enforcement_matrix.csv` — procedure, awards, annulment, recognition, and enforcement.
- `data/sequence_patch_arbitration.csv` — sequence correction showing this article between regional pluralism and compliance/enforcement.
- `notes/editorial_notes.md` — editorial and navigation notes.
- `sql/international_arbitration_schema.sql` — simple schema for cataloguing arbitration forms, authorities, and workflow steps.
- `python/build_international_arbitration_outputs.py` — builds structured JSON support outputs from CSV files.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON summaries and indexes.

## Editorial note

This article should be treated as a full article, not a subsection of courts or compliance. Arbitration is a distinct dispute-settlement tradition: consent-based, procedurally flexible, institutionally plural, and central to peaceful settlement, investor-state disputes, interstate claims, mixed claims commissions, and commercial enforcement involving states.
