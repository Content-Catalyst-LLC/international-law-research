# The High Seas, Deep Seabed Governance, and the Global Commons

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst / Content Catalyst article **The High Seas, Deep Seabed Governance, and the Global Commons**.

The public article explains high-seas freedoms, deep seabed governance, the common heritage of humankind, the International Seabed Authority, the BBNJ Agreement, marine biodiversity, environmental impact assessment, benefit sharing, precaution, technology transfer, and the legal governance of ocean commons beyond national jurisdiction.

## Folder structure

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — title, slug, focus keyword, excerpt, tags, and series metadata.
- `data/` — structured article matrices.
- `references/` — primary authorities and further reading.
- `sql/schema.sql` — article-support schema for structured notes.
- `python/` — small local helpers for generated outputs and link inventory.
- `outputs/` — generated summaries and Catalyst Data export row.
