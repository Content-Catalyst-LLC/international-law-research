# Editorial Notes — Self-Determination, Decolonization, and the Rights of Peoples

## Article role

This article follows **Territory, Borders, and Boundary Disputes in International Law** and precedes **Secession, Recognition, and Contested Statehood**.

## Editorial frame

Treat self-determination as both doctrine and anti-colonial legal claim. The article should explain the UN Charter, decolonization, Common Article 1 of the ICCPR and ICESCR, internal and external self-determination, territorial integrity, permanent sovereignty over natural resources, Indigenous peoples, and contested contemporary applications.

## Critical emphasis

Keep Palestine, Western Sahara, Chagos, Namibia, East Timor, Kashmir, Indigenous peoples, colonial boundaries, resource extraction, and unequal enforcement visible. Avoid presenting self-determination as either a simple secession rule or a purely historical decolonization doctrine.
