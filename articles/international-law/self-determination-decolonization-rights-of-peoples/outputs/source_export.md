https://www.un.org/dppa/decolonization/en/nsgt\n\n## Concepts\n- **Self-determination** — Right of peoples to determine political status and pursue economic social and cultural development\n- **Decolonization** — Legal and political process of ending colonial rule\n- **Internal self-determination** — Meaningful participation autonomy equality and cultural protection inside a state\n- **External self-determination** — Independence free association integration or other international status choice\n- **Territorial integrity** — Protection of existing states against external force and dismemberment\n- **Permanent sovereignty over natural resources** — Right of peoples and nations to control natural wealth and resources\n- **Non-Self-Governing Territories** — Territories whose people have not attained a full measure of self-government\n- **Indigenous self-determination** — Collective rights of Indigenous peoples to political status culture land and development\n
