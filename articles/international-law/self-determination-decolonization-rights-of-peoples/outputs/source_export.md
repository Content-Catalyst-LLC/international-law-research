## Concepts
- **Self-determination** — Right of peoples to determine political status and pursue economic social and cultural development
- **Decolonization** — Legal and political process of ending colonial rule
- **Internal self-determination** — Meaningful participation autonomy equality and cultural protection inside a state
- **External self-determination** — Independence free association integration or other international status choice
- **Territorial integrity** — Protection of existing states against external force and dismemberment
- **Permanent sovereignty over natural resources** — Right of peoples and nations to control natural wealth and resources
- **Non-Self-Governing Territories** — Territories whose people have not attained a full measure of self-government
- **Indigenous self-determination** — Collective rights of Indigenous peoples to political status culture land and development
