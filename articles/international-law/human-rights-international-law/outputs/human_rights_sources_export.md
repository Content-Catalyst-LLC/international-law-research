## Human Rights Concepts

- **Human dignity** (foundational principle) — Core premise that each person possesses inherent worth and rights.
- **Universal Declaration of Human Rights** (foundational instrument) — 1948 declaration articulating global human-rights standards.
- **International Bill of Human Rights** (source cluster) — UDHR ICCPR and ICESCR together.
- **Civil and political rights** (right category) — Rights associated with life liberty participation expression due process and political freedom.
- **Economic social and cultural rights** (right category) — Rights associated with work health education housing food culture and social security.
- **Progressive realization** (implementation doctrine) — ICESCR obligation to move toward full realization using maximum available resources.
- **Non-discrimination** (cross-cutting principle) — Equality obligation prohibiting unjust distinctions in rights enjoyment.
- **Self-determination** (peoplehood right) — Right of peoples to determine political status and development.
- **Treaty bodies** (monitoring mechanism) — Independent expert committees monitoring core human-rights treaties.
- **Human Rights Council** (Charter-based mechanism) — UN intergovernmental body responsible for promoting and protecting human rights.
- **Universal Periodic Review** (Charter-based mechanism) — Peer-review mechanism examining human-rights records of UN member states.
- **Regional human-rights systems** (regional mechanism) — European Inter-American and African systems protecting rights through regional institutions.
- **Customary human-rights law** (source of law) — Rights norms binding through custom regardless of treaty ratification.
- **Jus cogens** (peremptory norm) — Non-derogable norms such as prohibitions of genocide slavery torture and apartheid.
- **State responsibility** (legal consequence) — Responsibility of states for breaches of international human-rights obligations.
- **Palestine** (contemporary crisis) — Central case involving occupation self-determination apartheid allegations and human-rights protection.
- **Climate and human rights** (emerging field) — Human-rights implications of climate change environmental degradation and ecological harm.
- **Digital surveillance** (emerging field) — Privacy expression association and equality implications of surveillance and platform power.
- **Migration and displacement** (protection field) — Rights of refugees migrants stateless persons and displaced communities.
- **Corporate accountability** (governance field) — Human-rights responsibilities and accountability questions concerning business enterprises.
