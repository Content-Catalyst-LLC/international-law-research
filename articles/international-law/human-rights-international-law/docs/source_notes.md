## Critical framing

The article should avoid presenting human rights as a neutral Western gift to the world. It should foreground postwar legal development, anti-colonial struggle, Black internationalism, feminist struggle, Indigenous claims, disability justice, Global South legal agency, Palestine, and the uneven politics of enforcement. Human rights should be treated as a contested legal language of dignity, equality, social provision, and freedom from domination.
