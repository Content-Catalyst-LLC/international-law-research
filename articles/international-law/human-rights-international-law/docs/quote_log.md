# Quote Log — Human Rights in International Law

Use this file to track short primary-source excerpts used in the article.

## Current quote candidates

### UN Charter Article 1(3)

Excerpt: “promoting and encouraging respect for human rights”

Editorial purpose: Anchors human rights in the purposes of the United Nations.

### UN Charter Article 55

Excerpt: “universal respect for, and observance of, human rights and fundamental freedoms”

Editorial purpose: Shows that the Charter constitutionalized human-rights promotion.

### UDHR Article 1

Excerpt: “All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.”

Editorial purpose: Anchors human dignity and equality.

### UDHR Article 2

Excerpt: “without distinction of any kind”

Editorial purpose: Anchors non-discrimination.

### ICCPR Article 1

Excerpt: “All peoples have the right of self-determination.”

Editorial purpose: Links human rights to peoples, political status, and decolonization.

### ICCPR Article 6

Excerpt: “Every human being has the inherent right to life.”

Editorial purpose: Anchors right to life.

### ICESCR Article 2

Excerpt: “to the maximum of its available resources”

Editorial purpose: Anchors progressive realization and resource obligations.

### ICESCR Article 11

Excerpt: “adequate standard of living”

Editorial purpose: Anchors social rights.

### ICERD Article 1

Excerpt: “racial discrimination shall mean any distinction, exclusion, restriction or preference”

Editorial purpose: Anchors anti-racism as a core treaty obligation.

### Convention against Torture Article 2

Excerpt: “No exceptional circumstances whatsoever”

Editorial purpose: Anchors absolute prohibition of torture.

### African Charter Article 20

Excerpt: “All peoples shall have right to existence.”

Editorial purpose: Links human rights and peoples’ rights.

### ICJ South Africa v. Israel

Excerpt: “Application of the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide in the Gaza Strip”

Editorial purpose: Identifies the contemporary ICJ proceedings brought by South Africa.
