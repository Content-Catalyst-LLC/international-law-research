# Human Rights in International Law

Companion research folder for the Human Rights in International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UN Charter, UDHR, ICCPR, ICESCR, and International Bill of Human Rights source metadata
- core human-rights treaty and treaty-body notes
- regional human-rights system notes
- customary human-rights and jus cogens notes
- Palestine, Gaza, apartheid, racial domination, colonialism, and unequal protection notes
- digital surveillance, migration, climate, and corporate power notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for human-rights research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
