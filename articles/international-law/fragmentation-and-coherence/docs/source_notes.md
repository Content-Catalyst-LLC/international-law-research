## Critical framing

Fragmentation should not be treated as only a technical conflict-of-norms problem. Regime interaction is also shaped by institutional strength, enforceability, access, expertise, geopolitical power, and the unequal weight given to trade, investment, security, environmental, labor, human-rights, Indigenous, decolonial, and community-interest claims.
