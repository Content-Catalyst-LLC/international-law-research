# State Immunity, Diplomatic Immunity, and the Legal Protection of Official Functions

Companion research folder for the State Immunity, Diplomatic Immunity, and the Legal Protection of Official Functions article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations source metadata
- Vienna Convention on Consular Relations source metadata
- UN Convention on Jurisdictional Immunities of States and Their Property notes
- Convention on Internationally Protected Persons notes
- ICJ Arrest Warrant, Jurisdictional Immunities, Tehran Hostages, and Certain Iranian Assets case notes
- ILC immunity of State officials materials
- Damascus consular strike source notes
- quote logs
- conceptual mapping of state immunity, diplomatic immunity, consular immunity, ratione personae, ratione materiae, waiver, exceptions, and accountability tensions
- SQL schema for immunity-law research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
