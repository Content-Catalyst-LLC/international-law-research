# The Prohibition on the Use of Force in International Law

This article folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article on the prohibition on the use of force.

## Contents

- `docs/wordpress_article_html.html` — WordPress-ready article HTML.
- `data/primary_sources.csv` — primary legal authorities and institutional materials.
- `data/secondary_sources.csv` — selected scholarly and reference works.
- `data/use_of_force_authorities.csv` — doctrinal authorities and their analytical role.
- `data/exceptions_framework.csv` — lawful-force pathways and limits.
- `data/case_matrix.csv` — leading ICJ and institutional authorities.
- `python/build_use_of_force_matrix.py` — exports structured matrices as JSON.
- `python/audit_links.py` — extracts article URLs for review.
- `sql/queries.sql` — example queries for research use.
- `outputs/article_summary.json` — article-level metadata summary.
