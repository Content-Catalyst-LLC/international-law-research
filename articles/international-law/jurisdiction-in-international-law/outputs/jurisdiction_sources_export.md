## Jurisdiction Concepts

- **Jurisdiction** (core doctrine) — Legal competence to prescribe adjudicate and enforce law.
- **Prescriptive jurisdiction** (jurisdiction type) — Authority to make rules applicable to persons conduct or things.
- **Adjudicative jurisdiction** (jurisdiction type) — Authority of courts or tribunals to decide disputes.
- **Enforcement jurisdiction** (jurisdiction type) — Authority to compel compliance through coercive measures.
- **Territoriality** (jurisdiction basis) — Primary jurisdictional basis tied to state territory.
- **Subjective territoriality** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over conduct that begins within state territory.
- **Objective territoriality** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over conduct outside territory producing substantial effects inside it.
- **Effects doctrine** (jurisdiction basis) — Regulatory claim based on substantial domestic effects.
- **Nationality** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over nationals abroad.
- **Corporate nationality** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over corporations through incorporation seat headquarters listing or control links.
- **Passive personality** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction based on nationality of victim.
- **Protective principle** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over external conduct threatening core state interests.
- **Universal jurisdiction** (jurisdiction basis) — Jurisdiction over certain grave offenses regardless of territorial or nationality links.
- **Extraterritorial reach** (jurisdiction field) — Exercise of authority over persons conduct or effects outside national territory.
- **Lotus principle** (jurisprudential marker) — Classical principle that restrictions on state independence are not presumed.
- **Immunities** (jurisdiction limit) — Doctrines restricting foreign jurisdiction over states diplomats consuls and certain officials.
- **Human-rights jurisdiction** (treaty jurisdiction) — Question whether human-rights obligations apply to persons under a state's authority or effective control abroad.
- **Maritime jurisdiction** (sectoral jurisdiction) — Jurisdiction allocated by maritime zones and vessel status under UNCLOS.
- **Digital jurisdiction** (emerging field) — Jurisdiction over data platforms cyber operations and networked harms.
- **Enforcement abroad** (jurisdiction limit) — Physical coercive action in foreign territory requiring consent or legal authorization.
- **Jurisdiction and power** (critical method) — Analysis of how powerful states project jurisdiction through markets finance military reach and digital infrastructure.
