# Jurisdiction in International Law

Companion research folder for the Jurisdiction in International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- jurisdiction taxonomy notes
- Lotus and Arrest Warrant source metadata
- UN Charter and Friendly Relations source notes
- UNCLOS maritime-jurisdiction materials
- OHCHR extraterritorial human-rights jurisdiction materials
- universal jurisdiction notes
- corporate nationality and extraterritorial regulation notes
- cyber, sanctions, maritime enforcement, and digital jurisdiction notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for jurisdiction research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
