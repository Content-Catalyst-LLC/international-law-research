# Quote Log — Jurisdiction in International Law

Use this file to track short primary-source excerpts used in the article.

## Current quote candidates

### Lotus

Excerpt: “Restrictions upon the independence of States cannot therefore be presumed”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the classical permissive baseline often associated with jurisdiction.

### UN Charter Article 2(1)

Excerpt: “sovereign equality of all its Members”

Editorial purpose: Connects jurisdiction to sovereign equality.

### UN Charter Article 2(4)

Excerpt: “refrain in their international relations from the threat or use of force”

Editorial purpose: Distinguishes enforcement abroad from lawful jurisdictional assertion.

### Arrest Warrant

Excerpt: “immunity from criminal jurisdiction and inviolability”

Editorial purpose: Shows that jurisdiction may exist in principle but be blocked by immunity.

### UNCLOS Article 92

Excerpt: “Ships shall sail under the flag of one State only”

Editorial purpose: Anchors flag-state jurisdiction.

### UNCLOS Article 2

Excerpt: “sovereignty of a coastal State extends”

Editorial purpose: Anchors territorial-sea jurisdiction.

### OHCHR extraterritorial jurisdiction analysis

Excerpt: “International human rights law recognises a number of ways”

Editorial purpose: Shows that human-rights jurisdiction can extend beyond territory under control, custody, force, or authority.

### OHCHR universal jurisdiction explainer

Excerpt: “Universal jurisdiction is a legal principle”

Editorial purpose: Anchors the doctrine in accessible official human-rights guidance.
