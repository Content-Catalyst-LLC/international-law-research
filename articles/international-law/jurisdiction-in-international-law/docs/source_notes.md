## Critical framing

The article should explain jurisdiction with doctrinal precision while showing that jurisdiction is also a structure of power. States with reserve currencies, major markets, military reach, intelligence networks, and control over financial or digital infrastructure can project jurisdiction more effectively than weaker states. This asymmetry should be acknowledged without collapsing doctrine into politics.
