# Introduction to International Law

Companion research folder for the introductory International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UN Charter source metadata
- ICJ Statute Article 38 source notes
- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties materials
- ASIL everyday-life international law notes
- public/private international law distinction notes
- source taxonomy and doctrine mapping
- quote logs
- SQL schema for international-law introduction metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
