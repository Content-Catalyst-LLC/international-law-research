## Critical framing

The article should explain the Charter system precisely while refusing to sanitize its political structure. It should foreground the legal ambition of collective security and the fact that the veto and major-power impunity often prevent equal protection, especially in Palestine, Iraq, Yugoslavia/Kosovo, and other crises involving major powers or their allies.
