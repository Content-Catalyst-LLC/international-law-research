# United Nations Collective Security

Companion research folder for the United Nations Collective Security article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UN Charter collective-security source metadata
- Security Council Article 24, Article 27, Chapter VI, Chapter VII, Article 39, Article 41, Article 42, and Article 51 notes
- veto, permanent-member power, and Security Council paralysis notes
- UN peacekeeping legal-basis and principles notes
- Uniting for Peace and General Assembly workaround notes
- Palestine, Gaza, and South Africa v. Israel adjudication notes
- NATO, Iraq, Yugoslavia, selective enforcement, and double-standard notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for collective-security metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
