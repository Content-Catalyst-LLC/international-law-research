# Territory, Borders, and Boundary Disputes in International Law

Companion research folder for the Territory, Borders, and Boundary Disputes in International Law article in the International Law Foundations series.

## Purpose

This folder stores supporting material for the article, including:

- UN Charter territorial integrity source notes
- Island of Palmas source metadata
- ICJ Frontier Dispute and uti possidetis notes
- Temple of Preah Vihear notes
- Cameroon v. Nigeria / Bakassi notes
- Nicaragua v. Colombia territorial and maritime boundary notes
- Costa Rica v. Nicaragua navigational and territorial notes
- border doctrine and acquisition-of-territory mapping
- colonial boundaries and decolonization notes
- maritime boundary and UNCLOS notes
- quote logs
- SQL schema for territory and boundary-dispute research metadata
- lightweight Python utilities for exporting sources and validating metadata
- editorial notes for future article revisions
