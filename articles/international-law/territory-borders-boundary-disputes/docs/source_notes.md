## Critical framing

The article should not treat borders as neutral lines. Many modern borders are products of empire, war, colonial administration, forced migration, partition, cartography, resource extraction, and elite diplomacy. International law often preserves inherited boundaries to prevent conflict, but that stability can freeze injustice for indigenous peoples, divided communities, occupied peoples, stateless nations, and marginalized borderlands.
