# Gender, Violence, and the Public/Private Divide in International Law

This folder supports the Sustainable Catalyst International Law article:

**Gender, Violence, and the Public/Private Divide in International Law**

The folder is intentionally limited to research and support materials. It does **not** store the full WordPress HTML article. WordPress remains the publication layer; GitHub remains the research, metadata, source, and structured-output layer.

## Contents

- `metadata/article_metadata.json` — title, slug, sequence, SEO metadata, and repository status.
- `sources/primary_authorities.md` — primary instruments, cases, treaty materials, and institutional documents.
- `sources/further_reading.md` — scholarly and institutional reading list.
- `data/public_private_divide_matrix.csv` — public/private divide categories and legal reframing.
- `data/gender_based_violence_frameworks.csv` — international legal frameworks for gender-based violence analysis.
- `data/due_diligence_workflow.csv` — state responsibility and due-diligence workflow.
- `data/procedural_risk_matrix.csv` — evidentiary, credibility, remedy, and survivor-protection risks.
- `sql/gender_violence_schema.sql` — simple schema for cataloguing issue areas, authorities, duties, and remedies.
- `python/build_gender_violence_outputs.py` — builds structured JSON support outputs from the CSV files.
- `outputs/` — generated JSON summaries and indexes.

## Editorial note

This article examines how gender-based violence challenges the boundary between public authority and private life in international law. It treats domestic violence, sexual violence, reproductive coercion, trafficking, forced marriage, harmful practices, workplace exploitation, conflict-related sexual violence, and other gendered harms as legal problems shaped by state action, state inaction, discrimination, institutional design, due diligence, evidence, procedure, and remedy.
