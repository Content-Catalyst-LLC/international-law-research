# Occupation, Detention, and the Legal Governance of War-Torn Territory

This folder supports the article **Occupation, Detention, and the Legal Governance of War-Torn Territory** in the International Law series.

It contains source metadata, matrices, doctrine notes, SQL queries, helper scripts, and the WordPress article HTML used to keep the public article and the GitHub research repository aligned.

## Folder structure

```text
articles/international-law/occupation-detention-legal-governance-war-torn-territory/
  README.md
  metadata.json
  data/
  docs/
  python/
  sql/
  outputs/
```

## Core themes

- Hague occupation law
- Geneva Convention IV and protected persons
- Geneva Convention III and prisoners of war
- Common Article 3 and non-international armed conflict detention
- Internment, administrative detention, fair trial, ICRC access, deportation, transfer, property, settlements, humanitarian relief, and occupation accountability
