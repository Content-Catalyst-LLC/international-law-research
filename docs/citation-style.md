# Citation Style

The International Law knowledge series uses a Harvard-style reference format adapted for web publication.

## General Pattern

Author or Institution (Year) Title. Available at: URL.

## Treaty Example

United Nations (1969) Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties. Available at: https://legal.un.org/avl/ha/vclt/vclt.html.

## Court Example

International Court of Justice (1949) Corfu Channel (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland v. Albania), Merits, Judgment. Available at: https://www.icj-cij.org/case/1.

## Institutional Source Example

International Committee of the Red Cross (n.d.) Customary International Humanitarian Law Database. Available at: https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/en/customary-ihl.

## Scholarly Book Example

Anghie, A. (2004) Imperialism, Sovereignty and the Making of International Law. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

## Web Article Citation Guidance

When citing institutional web pages, include:

- Institution name
- Year if available, otherwise n.d.
- Page title
- URL
- Access date only if needed for unstable pages

## Internal Article Notes

Article drafts should maintain a distinction between:

- Further Reading: books, treatises, scholarly works, and broad learning resources
- References: sources directly relied upon in the article
