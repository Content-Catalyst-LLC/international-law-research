# Reuse and Licensing

This repository is designed for reusable legal research infrastructure.

## What May Be Reused

- SQL schema
- SQL views
- Lightweight Python scripts
- Original article-roadmap metadata
- Original documentation
- Citation and source hierarchy templates

## What Should Not Be Republished Here in Full

- Full treaty texts from official repositories
- Full judgments or advisory opinions
- Full institutional reports
- Full copyrighted academic chapters, articles, or monographs
- Proprietary database exports

## Preferred Practice

Use metadata, summaries, and authoritative links. Preserve source provenance and direct readers to official sources.
